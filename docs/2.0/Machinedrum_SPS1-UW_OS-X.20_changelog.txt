* Machinedrum OS X.20 C changelog *
-----------------------------------

This list covers the changes and bug fixes made after X.20 B
(2026-10-09). For the full list of X.20 features, see
Machinedrum_SPS1-UW_OS-X.20_readme.txt.

New
---

- With +Drive, Machine Banks now have 48 USR slots instead of 16.

- With +Drive, MACHINE MGR > INTERNAL can free the TRX, EFM or P-I machine
  categories for the selected Machine Bank, so that more USR Machines can be
  loaded. Choose FREE or LOAD in the MODE column and press [YES] to apply it
  to the selected category. [FUNCTION] + [CLEAR] also frees the selected
  category.

- With +Drive, a USR Machine copied in MACHINE MGR can now be pasted into
  another Machine Bank.

- The MD now calculates the DSP load for each machine. The total load is shared
  across the Kit rather than limited per track. Assigning a USR Machine
  that would overload the DSP shows "DSP LIMIT". Hold [SCALE] in EDIT KIT
  to see the Kit's total DSP load as a percentage.

- The MDS specification is now version 1.1. It gives each USR Machine
  separate DSP cycle figures for MKI and MKII. Existing USR Machines still
  load, but their DSP usage is only an estimate and can read too high or too
  low. A Kit may show "DSP LIMIT" too early, or pass the safety check and
  still overload the DSP. Update them with the certifier in the mds-tools
  repository.

- The bundled USR Machines have improved sound and performance.
  Send them to the MD again to apply the updates.

- Twelve new USR Machines: TXZ-SC, TXZ-CE, TXZ-SS, TXZ-BB, TXZ-EO, TXZ-FL,
  TX6-BD, TX6-SD, TX6-XT, TX6-CH, TX6-OH and TX6-CY.

- REC QUANT in Global SYNC > SEQUENCER, below TEMPO (formerly TEMPO
  CHANGE). Set it to OFF to keep the timing of trigs recorded live, as
  microtiming. ON, the default, places them on the nearest step, as
  before.

- LFO PAGE setting in Global CONTROL > ENGINE sets the LFO menu's key mapping.
  NEW, the default, works as before: [SYNTHESIS/EFFECTS/ROUTING] key
  cycles the LFO-A, LFO-B and ENV pages, and its LEDs show the open page.

  With LFO PAGE set to LEGACY, the [SYNTHESIS/EFFECTS/ROUTING] key closes the LFO
  menu and [LEFT]/[RIGHT] changes page. Its LEDs still show the open page.


Sequencer timing and performance
--------------------------------

- TIMING (Global CONTROL > ENGINE): PRECISE, the default, keeps trigs,
  microtiming and retrigs aligned with their programmed timing.
  LEGACY starts each trig as soon as the sound engine is ready, so its
  timing varies more.

- Improved sequencer timing, with less variation between trigs in PRECISE.

- With external MIDI clock, the sequencer now follows the clock more
  closely. In PRECISE, trigs after the first step reach the audio output
  sooner after each clock, and vary much less, than in X.14 or X.20 B.

- Reduced the first-step delay when starting from external MIDI clock,
  including when the clock master stops sending clock while paused.

- Improved response to live MIDI notes.

- Loading a row in MCL while playing no longer delays the first trigs
  of the new row.

- The screen responds faster to page changes while playing.

- RTIM: 1/128 is now the fastest sequencer retrig interval.

+Drive
------

- Saving and loading +Drive snapshots is faster.

- Loading a Machine Bank from +Drive, at power-on or when changing banks,
  is several times faster.

Fixes
-----

** Sequencer and MIDI sync **

- Starting from external MIDI clock could leave the pattern one clock
  late until Stop. Early clock pulses are now retained during Start.

- Devices following Machinedrum's MIDI clock could start slightly out of
  step with it. Playback now begins on a clock, with START or CONTINUE
  sent just before it.

- On rare occasions, playback could stop by itself.

- A pattern change from a Program Change, a MIDI note or the panel had
  to arrive about two steps before the end of the playing pattern, or
  it waited a whole extra pattern. Arriving a little over one step
  before the end is now enough.

- Song mode ignored a row's BPM unless TEMPO was set to PATTERN,
  and rows with no BPM (--) ignored the pattern's tempo. A row's BPM now
  always applies, and a row with no BPM uses the pattern's tempo when
  Global SYNC > SEQUENCER > TEMPO is set to PATTERN.

- Extended mode: pasting a pattern kept the old Kit number, so reloading
  the Kit brought back the old sounds and SAVE KIT offered the old slot.
  A pasted pattern now brings its Kit number with it, and undo restores
  the previous one.

- Extended mode: switching to a pattern with no trigs loaded the Kit
  stored in it, usually Kit 01, instead of keeping the current Kit.

- With a trig held, the first [LEFT]/[RIGHT] press or track-select wheel
  turn only opens the microtiming popup. Later presses and turns change
  the value.

** Sound and machines **

- A loud pop could be heard at power-on, around the end of the splash
  screen. Delay, Reverb, EQ and Dynamics settings now glide to new values,
  as in X.14, instead of jumping.

- AMD and AMF could stop working correctly on a track once its DIST had
  been above 0, until power-off.

- After power-on, SRR could have no effect for several seconds, or until
  it was turned up high once.

- With TUNING in Global CONTROL > ENGINE at +5 cents or more, the highest
  notes on ROM and RAM-P tracks could freeze the MD.

- RAM-R machines recorded nothing, and pasting from ROM to RAM showed
  ERROR, after power-on.

- Looped UserWave samples looped at the wrong point.

- Changing VOL, or another routing setting, on one track could silence
  another track playing an NFX or user machine. The track stayed silent
  until its own setting was changed.

- Muting an NFX or INP track did not silence it or its effect sends.

- A parameter modulated by an LFO could stay at a modulated value, such
  as VOL at 0, after the LFO moved to another destination or its depth
  went to 0.

- MID tracks could send wrong CC values while other tracks used
  parameter slides.

- Auditioning a sample or machine in Sample Manager, MACHINE MGR or EDIT
  KIT could play it with the track's own pitch and settings instead of its
  defaults.

- Pressing STOP twice did not silence a user machine that was still
  sounding.

- A USR Machine that fails its check when received is now refused by
  itself. The others in the send are still saved.

** CTR-AL and CTR-8P **

- CTR-AL: changes made through a CTR-AL track are now kept in the Kit, so
  they are shown when editing and are included when the Kit is saved,
  sent or copied.

- CTR-AL and CTR-8P: previewing a held step no longer changes the
  parameters of the tracks they control.

- CTR-AL: knob moves recorded live are recorded on the CTR-AL track
  only, not on every track it controls.

- CTR-AL: an LFO aimed at its LFOS, LFOD or LFOM parameter now takes
  effect, and editing another LFO no longer brings back an old CTR-AL
  value over a parameter changed by hand.

** Kits, banks and +Drive **

- EDIT KIT: choosing another machine for a track reset its tuning to
  DEFAULT. As in X.14, the track now keeps its tuning when the new
  machine supports tonal tuning.

- Kits converted from X.14, at first boot or from an X.14 snapshot, lost
  their TONAL settings.

- SAVE KIT reopened the Kit list after [YES] was pressed on the name.
  SAVE SONG could skip a save.

- The Snapshot, Sample Bank and Machine Bank managers opened at the first
  slot, or wherever the cursor was last left, instead of at the active
  slot.

- A +Drive whose Machine Bank area had never been used could show BANK
  ERROR with no slots.

- Selecting a damaged or empty Machine Bank unloaded the bank in use.

** Panel and display **

- A bank LED could mark a pattern as used when it had no trigs or
  parameter locks, for example after its tracks were cleared.

- BANK GROUP was not kept at power-off, so the LEDs showed A-D after
  starting on an E-H pattern.

- In step editing mode, the playing step's LED could stay over the bank
  and mute views, making an empty pattern look used or a used one look
  empty.

- In step editing mode, a step could stay held after its trig was
  released, for example when [SCALE] changed page while it was held. Its
  parameter locks then showed on other pages, and knob moves changed its
  locks instead of the sound. [SCALE] no longer changes page while a trig
  is held.

- The 3/4x and 3/2x track speeds were labelled the wrong way round.

- Pressing an encoder did not show its value on the main, LFO, master
  effect and trigger input pages, as it does in X.14.

- [FUNCTION] + knob did not move the other tracks when the selected
  track's value was already at its minimum or maximum, for example on an
  empty GND track.

- A key such as [FUNCTION] could stay held after it was released, so
  trig presses acted as [FUNCTION] + trig.

- A worn encoder could lock out all keys and knobs until power-off.
